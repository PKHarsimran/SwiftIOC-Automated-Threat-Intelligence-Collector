How to read this evidence

These are associations reported by ransomware.live. They do not establish current use, local exposure, a shared campaign, or collaboration between groups. Group techniques describe the group’s reported behavior; they are not automatically attributed to each IOC.

“In SwiftIOC” means an exact record match in the retained feed. Research candidates do not automatically enter scores or blocklists. An empty result does not prove a group has no such activity.
